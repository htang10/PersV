An AI-powered querying tool that allows anyone from any background, including non-technical, to extract information from their database with pure natural language. Bring your data, ask questions, and instantly get actionable insights.💡


## Features

- **Ask in plain English:** retrieve information with natural language
- **Quick demo:** onboard instantly, no signup required
- **Bring your own data:** connect your own data source beyond the built-in demo
- **Persistent activity history:** registered users get a full audit trail of past questions, answers, and generated scripts
- **Smart caching:** repeated queries return instantly without reprocessing
